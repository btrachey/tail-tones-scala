# S17 spikes — continuous pentatonic bed drone

User request (2026-11-11): the default builtin bed becomes ONE constant tone
that slowly moves between the pentatonic pitches randomly; brief overlap
during transitions is fine; octave offsets are fine. This file records the
ground truths that shaped the implementation.

## A. One-shot voices cannot carry a continuous tone (ring math)

`PcmRing.CapacityFrames = 4·4410` (~400 ms) and `offer` is drop-newest, never
blocking (PcmRing.scala). `PcmMixer.playVoice` renders the WHOLE voice then
does ONE `offer`: a 3–6 s drone sample = 132k–264k frames into a 17,640-frame
ring → everything past ~400 ms is silently dropped. A scheduler-level `durMs`
change therefore degrades to "still short bursts, just truncated" on
coreaudio/ffplay-pipe — the primary backends. Conclusion: the tone must be
generated on the CONSUMER side (drain/fill thread), not offered as samples.

## B. The drone is additive, never a priority slot

Drain sites pick frames `ring → bedLoop → silence`. Putting the drone behind
that chain gates it on ring emptiness. Against shipped defaults
(`minIntervalMs=150`, `maxPolyphony=3`, `Alerts.DurMs=180`), an elevated-
severity burst = 3×180 ms = 540 ms of alert audio against a 400 ms ring: the
ring stays non-empty for most of each burst window, i.e. exactly when
follow-mode runs for hours, the bed would pulse on/off. The ring is also FIFO
— queued notes are never preempted — so nothing else "makes room". Fix shape
(shipped): `PcmMixer.mixDrone(frames, n)` sums the drone into whatever was
chosen (saturating int16) BEFORE `applyMasterFade`, at all three drain sites
(PipeEngine.mixerLoop, CoreAudioEngine.pollOnce + finalFlush), and the
oscillators advance EVERY frame regardless of gain (mute/duck included) so
glide/pitch state never desyncs from wall time. Pinned by
`PcmMixerDroneSpec."additive over the ring"` (ring holds 4×441 zero frames —
the worst-case "ring non-empty" — and the tone must still reach the sink).

## C. Gain rides the mix, pitch is the only published state

An early design carried `gain` on the published target. Wrong: ducking is
already folded into `bedGain` by `Severity.derive` (§7.1,
`bedVolume·(1−duckMax·S)`) and mute is `MutingEngine` forwarding
`MixParams(0,0,0,0)` — a second gain channel snapshotted at pitch-change time
(every ~5 s) would keep the drone audible THROUGH mute and lag duck by a full
dwell. Shipped: `DroneTarget(freqHz: Float)` only; `mixDrone` reads
`paramsRef.bedGain` live, quantizes at `bed.bits` at full scale, then scales
by bedGain in int16 (quantize-then-scale, the docs/spikes/S10.md lesson —
pre-quantization gain collapses coarse-bit buckets).

## D. Capability is a side trait; the predicate resolves through the decorator

`AudioEngine` is plan §6.1 VERBATIM — capability went in as a separate trait
(`DroneCapable`, the `ChildOwned` precedent), NOT as a defaulted method.
Main wraps EVERY engine in `MutingEngine`, so the decorator MUST answer
`sustainsDrone` by interrogating its delegate: constant-true would route
spawn's bed to a no-op `setDroneTarget` → SILENT bed (not the arpeggiator
fallback); omitting the methods would deny the drone to coreaudio/ffplay in
production entirely. Scheduler resolves once at construction
(`case d: DroneCapable if d.sustainsDrone`). Pinned by
ModeSignalSpec."DroneCapable resolves through MutingEngine" + the
wrapped-null / wrapped-spawn scheduler pair (spawn-side proof reads the
scheduler EMIT stream — `voice-start source=bed` present, `bed-drone` absent
— because spawn's unstarted `playVoice` swallows everything and engine-side
observation would pass vacuously).

## E. Two-voice crossfade, phase-continuous

Retarget = re-tune the quieter voice to the audible one's pitch, COPY its
phases (starts in unison: no phase discontinuity), sweep weights linearly
over `droneGlideMs` while its pitch slews old→new. Both oscillators advance
every frame → the overlap the user asked for is real and neither voice ever
starts mid-cycle (click-free). A mid-glide retarget COMMITS the in-flight
crossfade (true weights/pitches) before choosing in/out voices, so an
interrupted glide lands on the LATEST target — pinned by
`PcmMixerDroneSpec."mid-glide retarget"`.

## F. Determinism

`DroneWalker` owns its own `Random(seed ^ 0x0d20ea7c)` (the Arpeggiator
pattern): 1 draw at construction, exactly 4 per `next()`, jump never 0
(±1..±3 pool steps mod 10) so consecutive pitches always differ. The alert
lottery RNG (`seed ^ 0x5deece66d`) never sees walker draws → storm/alert
goldens keep their shapes; only the healthy/warnDrift streams changed
(`voice-start source=bed` × N → a few `bed-drone` lines). NullEngine records
every publish as `drone freqHz=<f>`; `--print-events` shows `kind=bed-drone`.

## G. Human listening — UNVERIFIED-BY-AGENT

The CI box's audio capture is TCC-silent; as with S10–S16, ear checks are the
user's. `scripts/verify-audio.sh` step 1 accepts either `kind=bed-drone`
(streaming default) or `voice-start source=bed` (spawn grid) as "bed is on".
Listen matrix: tone constant (no gaps/pulses under alert bursts), transitions
smooth (glide audible, no click/zip at crossfade start or landing), octave
jumps musically sane, duck/mute still take effect ≤ ~50 ms.
